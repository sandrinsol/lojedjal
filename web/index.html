Early build of Stage I, the Drowned Abbey. Somnolent Monks bar the corridors: step in front of one to duel. He raises his censer and holds it, slowly and unevenly. Press S to block and the blow lands softly. Wait until he flashes white, just before the blow lands, and the same press is a perfect parry: he staggers, and your next strike cuts twice as deep. Dying brands your arm and returns you to the gate. Touch controls appear on phones. Runs in the browser with EmulatorJS and the mGBA core (GPL-3.0; see NOTICE.md).
