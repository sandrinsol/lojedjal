SEPTEM

Click the game once so it takes the keyboard.

Arrow keysStep forward / back, turn left / right
X keyA: strike with the sword
EnterStart
ShiftSelect: take a wound (the 4th wound kills and brands the arm)
A keyL: spend a mana gem
S keyR: restore the gems

Early build: exploration of Stage I, the Drowned Abbey. Wounds, gems and the death brand are on preview keys until duels exist. Touch controls appear on phones. Runs in the browser with EmulatorJS and the mGBA core (GPL-3.0; see NOTICE.md).
